# Constitution of the Phoenix Engineering Operating System (PEOS)

**Artifact ID:** CONST-000001  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Ratified  
**Classification:** Constitutional Artifact

---

# Preamble

We establish the Phoenix Engineering Operating System (PEOS) to create a governed, reproducible, verifiable, self-describing, and continuously improving engineering platform.

PEOS exists to transform engineering knowledge into executable knowledge.

Every architectural decision, implementation, automation, verification, certification, and operational activity shall preserve the integrity of the system and improve its ability to build, govern, verify, and evolve itself.

---

# Article I — Architecture

Architecture is the primary artifact.

Implementation exists to realize the architecture.

If implementation conflicts with architecture, implementation shall stop until the architecture is reviewed.

---

# Article II — Modules

Everything managed by PEOS is a Module.

Every capability managed by the platform shall exist as a Module implementing the common Module Contract.

No capability shall become a special case.

---

# Article III — Kernel

Every interface is a client of the Kernel.

The Kernel coordinates engineering operations.

Business rules belong to Modules and system services, never to user interfaces.

---

# Article IV — Governance

Every significant engineering activity shall be governed.

Governance precedes implementation.

---

# Article V — Automation

No implementation without automation.

Automation shall be designed before implementation begins.

Repeated manual procedures shall evolve into Certified Tool Artifacts.

---

# Article VI — Certified Tools

Every architectural evolution shall be executable by a Certified Tool Artifact.

Certified Tools shall:

- Inspect
- Validate
- Plan
- Preview
- Execute
- Verify
- Certify
- Record
- Report

---

# Article VII — Verification

Nothing is trusted without verification.

Every Module, Tool, Work Order, and architectural evolution shall define its verification strategy before implementation.

---

# Article VIII — Activity

Every significant operation shall be recorded.

Activity records constitute the engineering history of PEOS.

---

# Article IX — Stewardship

PEOS assists engineers.

It does not replace engineering judgment.

Its purpose is to increase engineering capability through governance and automation.

---

# Article X — Evolution

PEOS evolves itself through governed, certified automation.

Every evolution shall increase the platform's ability to improve itself.

---

# Article XI — Simplicity

When multiple compliant solutions exist, the simplest solution that satisfies the Constitution shall be preferred.

Complexity requires justification.

---

# Article XII — Compatibility

Backward compatibility shall be intentional.

Breaking changes require architectural justification, governance approval, migration guidance, and verification.

---

# Engineering Covenant

We do not write software merely to make it work.

We engineer systems that explain themselves.

We automate engineering knowledge.

We govern engineering decisions.

We certify architectural evolution.

We record engineering history.

We preserve knowledge above implementation.

We leave the platform more understandable than we found it.

---

# Engineering Oath

Every contributor affirms:

- I will not implement without architecture.
- I will not automate without governance.
- I will not repeat what can become a Certified Tool.
- I will protect the Constitution.
- I will improve the platform for the next engineer.

---

# The North Star

Every engineering decision shall answer one question:

**Will this make the next engineer more autonomous?**

If the answer is no, the decision shall be reconsidered.

---

# Closing Statement

The Constitution governs PEOS.

Every document, Module, Tool, Work Order, ADR, implementation, and release derives its authority from this Constitution.

Nothing in PEOS may intentionally contradict this document.
